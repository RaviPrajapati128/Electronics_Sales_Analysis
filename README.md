##  Project Overview
This project features a dynamic and interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** built using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard provides a high-level view of sales KPIs, enabling business stakeholders to track revenue trends, analyze product demand, and evaluate regional performance at a glance.

The analysis is based on a transactional dataset comprising over 1,000 records, covering diverse product categories across multiple global regions.

---

##  Key Features & Functionality
* **Executive Summary:** Real-time calculation of **Total Revenue**, **Total Units Sold**, and **Average Discount Rates**.
* **Time-Series Analysis:** A line chart visualizing monthly revenue trends to identify seasonality and growth patterns.
* **Product Deep-Dive:** * Breakdown of **Quantity Sold by Product** (Laptops, Headphones, Smartphones, etc.).
    * Revenue distribution by **Product Category** (Accessories, Electronics, Peripherals).
* **Geographic Insights:** Regional sales comparison highlighting market share across North America, South America, and Europe.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Fully interactive filters for **Region** and **Payment Method**, allowing users to drill down into specific data segments instantly.
