# Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
*This project features a comprehensive Sales Performance Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel. It transforms raw sales data into actionable insights, focusing on revenue trends, product performance, and regional sales distribution. The goal is to provide stakeholders with a clear visual representation of KPIs to drive data-driven decision-making.

## Key Features
*Total Metrics: Instant visibility into Total Revenue, Total Quantity Sold, and Average Discount rates.
*Trend Analysis: A monthly breakdown of revenue to identify seasonal peaks and growth patterns.
*Product Performance: Analysis of sales by category (Electronics, Accessories, Peripherals) and specific products (Laptops, Smartphones, etc.).
*Regional Insights: Comparative analysis of sales across North America, South America, and Europe.
*Interactive Slicers: Dynamic filtering by Region and Payment Method for granular data exploration.

## Dataset Description
The analysis is based on a dataset containing 1,000+ transactions with the following attributes:
*Order Details: Order ID, Date of Sale.
*Product Info: Product Name, Category.
*Financials: Revenue ($), Quantity Sold, Discount (%).
*Logistics: Salesperson, Region, and Payment Method.

## Tools Used
*Microsoft Excel: * Power Query: For data cleaning and transformation.
*Pivot Tables: For data aggregation and summarization.
*Data Visualization: Custom charts (Combo charts, Pie charts, and Bar graphs).

## Key Insights from the Data
*Top Category: Accessories and Electronics drive the highest volume of sales, with Laptops being a primary revenue contributor.
*Regional Leader: North America consistently shows the highest engagement across all product categories.
*Revenue Trends: There is a significant fluctuation in monthly revenue, suggesting opportunities for targeted marketing during "dip" periods.
